business insights¶

In [ ]:

Targeted Lifestyle Marketing over Demographics: Marital status is a strong indicator of pet ownership. Marketing campaigns for pet-related products or services should be personalized based on marital status rather than broad categories like age or state.

Gender-Neutral Pricing and Promotions: Since there is no statistically significant difference in spending between Male, Female, and Non-Binary customers, promotional offers and pricing models can remain gender-neutral without losing efficiency.

Universal Engagement Strategy: Older customers exhibit the same level of activity (Days Since Last Interaction) as younger ones. Digital engagement and loyalty programs should be inclusive of all age groups, as "age-related drop-off" is not present in this data.

National vs. Regional Campaigns: Because spending is uniform across states like Florida, Texas, and New York, the company can prioritize large-scale national marketing campaigns over expensive, localized regional adjustments.

Focus on Education-Agnostic Value: Education levels do not drive spending behavior in this dataset. This suggests that the product or service appeals to a wide variety of intellectual backgrounds, and messaging should focus on universal value propositions rather than professional prestige.
